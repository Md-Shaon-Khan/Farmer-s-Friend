Built to help farmers act early

Rice diseases such as blast and brown spot can reduce a harvest quickly, and they are easy to confuse with each other. Many farmers don't have an expert nearby when they need one.

Paddy Check gives a fast first opinion from a phone photo: the likely disease, the affected area, and similar cases, so treatment can start sooner.
