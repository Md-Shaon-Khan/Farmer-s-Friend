    About

    Most farmers can't call an expert every time they see a spot.

    Sometimes the officer is far away, or busy, and the crop does not wait. We wanted something that works from a phone photo and gives a first idea quickly. It is not a doctor, just a second pair of eyes to help you decide what to do next.
