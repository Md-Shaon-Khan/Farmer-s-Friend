Disease guide

Bacterial diseases

Bacteria enter through small wounds on the leaf and move with water. You see them more after heavy rain, strong wind or flooding.

Classify a leaf

What you may see

  • Water-soaked streaks, usually starting at the leaf tip or edge
  • Streaks turn yellow, then grey-white
  • Edges can look wavy
  • In the morning you may see small drops of ooze on the leaf

What farmers usually do

  • Don't put on too much nitrogen fertiliser
  • Drain the field if you can
  • Use clean seed and resistant varieties
  • Don't walk from sick plots into healthy ones when the field is wet
Examples

Common ones

Bacterial leaf blight

Xanthomonas oryzae
Looks like
Yellow-white streaks from the tip, leaves dry and curl
Good to know
Can empty a field fast in bad years, so check early

Bacterial leaf streak

Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzicola
Looks like
Thin brown streaks between the veins
Good to know
Looks like blight at first, the streaks stay narrow
