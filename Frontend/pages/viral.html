Disease guide

Viral diseases

Viruses get into the plant through insects that feed on it. Once a plant is infected you can't cure it, so the work is in stopping the insects and pulling out the sick plants early.

Classify a leaf

What you may see

  • Leaves turn yellow or orange, often from the tip down
  • Plants stay short and grow fewer tillers
  • Few grains, or grains that are empty
  • Patches in the field, not an even spread

What farmers usually do

  • Pull out and bury badly affected plants
  • Control the insect that carries it, mostly leafhoppers and planthoppers
  • Use resistant varieties if your area has them
  • Keep nearby weeds and old stubble clean
Examples

Common ones

Rice tungro

Spread by green leafhoppers
Looks like
Yellow to orange leaves, stunted plants
Good to know
Plant at the same time as your neighbours so insects don't move between stages

Grassy stunt

Spread by brown planthoppers
Looks like
Very short plants with thin, grass-like leaves
Good to know
Watch for planthoppers early in the season
