Disease guide

Fungal diseases

This is the biggest group. Fungi like damp and warm weather, and spores travel through the air and water. Most of them leave spots with a shape you can learn to recognise.

Classify a leaf

What you may see

  • Spots with a clear edge and a different colour in the middle
  • Spots that join into larger dead patches
  • Lesions on the leaf, the sheath or even the neck of the panicle
  • Worse after many cloudy, humid days

What farmers usually do

  • Use healthy seed
  • Space plants so the leaves can dry
  • Balance fertiliser, too much nitrogen makes it worse
  • Ask your officer about a fungicide that suits your area and stage
Examples

Common ones

Leaf blast

Magnaporthe oryzae
Looks like
Diamond-shaped spots, grey centre, brown edge
Good to know
Can spread to the neck of the panicle and ruin the grain

Brown spot

Bipolaris oryzae
Looks like
Small oval brown spots, often with a yellow ring
Good to know
Often shows up when plants are stressed or the soil is poor

Sheath blight

Rhizoctonia solani
Looks like
Greyish-green patches on the sheath near the water line
Good to know
Climbs up the plant in thick, wet crops
