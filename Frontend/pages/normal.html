Normal leaf

Sometimes the leaf is just fine, or the colour is off for a plain reason like age, shade or a nutrient problem. Here is what a healthy paddy leaf looks like.

Classify a leaf

What you may see

  • Even green colour, from base to tip
  • Smooth, long blade with no spots or streaks
  • Clean edges, not curled or dried
  • Plant stands upright and grows steadily

What farmers usually do

  • Keep checking every week or so
  • Take a photo of a healthy leaf to compare later
  • Keep water and fertiliser steady
  • If only the lowest old leaves turn yellow, that is usually just ageing
